time_in_principal_residence(_p1, _p2, _p3)
Reading

Whenever all of the following happen:

  • individual i gains gain from sale or exchange of their property p
  • t is the count of all d, grouping on i, p, such that the following is the case:
    • at some point within 5 years (included) in the past, the following happened:
      • all of the following are the case:
        • individual i uses property p as their principal residence
        • individual i owns property p
        • the current time is equal to cur_time
        • the day of time cur_time is equal to d

Then the following shall be constituted:

  • the amount of time that individual _p1 used property _p2 as their principal residence
