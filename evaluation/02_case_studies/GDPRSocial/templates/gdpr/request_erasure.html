Request Erasure (Art. 17)

You have the right to obtain the erasure of personal data concerning you (right to be forgotten).

What gets deleted?

If you choose "Delete ALL my data", the following will be permanently removed:

  • All your twits, replies, and reposts
  • All your likes
  • All your direct messages (sent and received)
  • Your follow relationships (followers and following)
  • Your consent records and GDPR notifications
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Activity & statistics data (page visits, logs)
  • Your name and email will be cleared from your profile

Note: Your username and account will remain so you can still log in, but all personal data will be erased. To delete a single item instead, choose the specific option below.
