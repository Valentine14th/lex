Contest Accuracy & Restrict Processing (Art. 16 + 18)

If you believe data we hold about you is inaccurate, you can contest its accuracy. While accuracy is being verified, processing of that data for the specified purpose will be restricted.

Under Art. 18(1)(a) GDPR, you have the right to obtain restriction of processing where you contest the accuracy of personal data, for a period enabling the controller to verify the accuracy.
