Request Rectification (Art. 16)

You have the right to obtain the rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning you.

How it works
  1. Select the field you want to correct (e.g. your name, email, or a specific twit).
  2. For twits, replies, or messages, enter the Object ID (visible on the item's page).
  3. Enter the corrected value and submit.
